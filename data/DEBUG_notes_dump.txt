*   **Regulatory & Environmental Policy (EU Focus):**
    *   The European Union drafted legislation requiring outside goods to disclose quarterly emission amounts.
    *   The specific mechanism discussed is the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM).
    *   For products entering the EU, the EU will study emissions data from January 2026 to December 2026 to set caps for each product.
    *   This process involves calculating emissions from suppliers bringing products into the EU boundary.
    *   The reporting period for data collection is specified as January 2026 to December 2026.
    *   The transition timeline for data collection and reporting is tied to October 20th, with data covering the two years prior and the current period.
    *   Products are bifurcated based on HSN codes and CN codes to determine allowed emission limits.
